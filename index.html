    At ACM-W, we are a community of passionate, curious, and driven women in technology, working to build an inclusive and supportive ecosystem on campus.

    Our core team unites engineering, UI/UX design, leadership, and event execution under one mission: empowering women to pursue, excel, and lead in computing. We believe in taking up space in tech — because you belong here.
